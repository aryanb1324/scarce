What this does and does not show. It shows that under this protocol kWTA is reliably worse, and worse in proportion to how short training was — not in proportion to how little data there was. Because epochs were held fixed while the dataset shrank, the 1% condition ran ~50 optimizer steps against ~4,690 at 100%, and kWTA updates roughly a fifth of the units per step. The harness gave the sparse model fewest steps exactly where it needed most. So this result cannot yet separate “sparsity hurts data efficiency” from “sparsity converges slower and was scored before it finished.” Matching gradient steps across budgets is the experiment that decides it.
